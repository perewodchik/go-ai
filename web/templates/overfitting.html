Overfitting Diagnostics

Every other chart in this app is built from the model playing itself, and self-play cannot see a weakness both sides share. This page measures the things that can: how much of the search survives into the training labels, how widely the policy head still spreads its prior, and — the only unambiguous one — how results against outside opponents compare with results against its own lineage.

Probing weights and replaying games…

Generalization gap

Win rate against its own ancestors and the random anchor (internal) versus anything from outside the training loop (external), bucketed by the iteration the games were played at. The two lines tracking each other is health. The internal line staying high while the external line falls is overfitting — and it is the one signal the promotion gate structurally cannot produce, because a network is never much worse than itself.

Same opponents, later iterations

The aggregate external rate confounds strength with scheduling — face easy bots early and hard ones later and you look like you declined. This splits each opponent's games at the median iteration, so every comparison is against the same opponent. A red delta is a real regression.

Policy entropy over training

The leading indicators, per iteration. Label entropy is how much of the search's judgement reaches the network — it narrows first if the policy target temperature is below 1. Network entropy is how widely the policy head actually spreads its prior over legal moves, and follows the labels down over the following tens of iterations. Both are in nats; higher is wider. Gaps mean those iterations ran before the metric was recorded.

Replay buffer label age profile

Label entropy across the buffer from oldest to newest samples. This is the one to watch after changing the policy target temperature: the buffer holds roughly thirty iterations, so a fix appears here as a rising right-hand edge long before the averages move. A flat line means every sample in the buffer, new and old, was built the same way.
